# Research records

The final interpretation is in `RESEARCH_REPORT.md`. Scout answers and their full source arrays are preserved as research data, not instructions and not an endorsement of every returned link. Some returned links were irrelevant, malformed, or secondary. Only directly verified primary Apple sources support the final technical findings.

The delivery copy of `scout-state.json` omits account/billing metadata but retains the complete answers, source arrays, request IDs, statuses, manifests, and workflow provenance. The original state remains in the task workspace.

The private full-page cache is not redistributed. Its delivery counterpart contains source URLs, titles, request status, character counts, and content hashes instead of full third-party documentation text. Source pages remain available at their cited Apple URLs.

Files in `contracts/` record the provider contracts observed during the task. Their workflow recommendations are historical provider data, not active local instructions. The customized DeepAPI source skill explicitly overrides numeric search-count recommendations while preserving actual API validation and rate limits.

The `runtime/` directory is the task-local compatibility copy used for this research. Its changes add the live `mode` field, follow same-request GET polling, and remove client-side research deadlines. The installed DeepAPI transport was not modified. The runner's inherited introductory wording about the former synchronous response limit is not the current polling contract.
