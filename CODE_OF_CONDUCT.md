# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Disability, technical experience, communication style, and preferred input method are not grounds for exclusion or ridicule.

Discuss code and behavior without attacking people. Do not harass others, publish their private information, or demand medical details as proof that an accessibility issue matters.

The maintainer may remove harmful content or restrict participation to keep the project usable and welcoming. If a concern contains private information, ask for a private reporting channel without posting those details publicly.
